# Sample Daycare

Fresh MVP rebuild. Keep the first version focused on the day-to-day daycare lunch workflow.

## Stack

- React, TypeScript, and Vite
- PocketBase, on a separate instance

## MVP scope

- CLC admins manage multiple daycare locations; each daycare staff account is limited to its own location.
- Manage students and classrooms.
- CLC can enable substitutions per daycare; CLC or daycare staff can manage substitutions.
- Print a US Letter kitchen sheet with weekday totals, school breakdowns, regular meals, substitutions, and pizza counts.
- Reuse weekly counts until schedules, rosters, substitutions, or closed days change.
- Create monthly menus, mark days off, publish a read-only public menu, and download PDF or Word copies.
- Project monthly invoice subtotals from service days and student counts, with notes and adjustments.

This repository is being rebuilt from a clean baseline; implementation will follow the approved spreadsheet examples.
